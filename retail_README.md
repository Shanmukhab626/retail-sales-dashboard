## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Retail Sales Performance Dashboard** analyzing **$32,847 in revenue** across 4 US regions, 3 product categories, and 50 orders over a 4-month period (January–April 2026).

Built using business intelligence concepts including KPI tracking, DAX-equivalent measures, dynamic filtering, and actionable business insight generation — modeled on Power BI dashboard methodology.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 🔴 Finding 1 — Furniture Underperforming
Furniture generates 35.2% of revenue but only 14.5% profit margin due to high discount rates (15–20%). **Recommendation**: Cap discounts at 10%, discontinue 3 bottom SKUs.

### 🟢 Finding 2 — Technology is Star Category
Technology drives 49.8% of revenue at consistent 22% margin with near-zero discounting. **Recommendation**: Expand Technology SKU range, prioritize stock in West and Central.

### 🟡 Finding 3 — Office Supplies Hidden Gem
Office Supplies deliver 43.9% margin — highest of all categories. **Recommendation**: Bundle with Technology orders to increase basket size.
