🔴 Finding 1 — Furniture Underperforming
Furniture category generates 35.2% of revenue but only 14.5% profit margin — the lowest across all categories. High discount rates (15–20%) are eroding margins. Recommendation: Cap furniture discounts at 10% and discontinue 3 bottom-performing SKUs.
🟢 Finding 2 — Technology is Star Category
Technology drives 49.8% of total revenue at a consistent 22% margin with near-zero discounting needed. Laptops and phones are top performers. Recommendation: Increase Technology SKU range and prioritize stock in West and Central regions where demand is highest.
🟡 Finding 3 — Office Supplies Hidden Gem
Office Supplies contribute only 15% of revenue but deliver 43.9% profit margin — highest of all categories. Low revenue due to low unit prices, but high volume potential. Recommendation: Bundle Office Supplies with Technology orders to increase basket size and overall profitability.
